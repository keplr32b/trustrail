INCIDENT REPORT — Laptop Damage Claim #IC-2026-0819

Customer: J. Kappy
Device: 14" laptop, purchased under accident-protection plan

Description of incident:
The laptop was accidentally knocked off a desk on August 15, 2026,
resulting in visible physical damage to the display. The screen shows
a large diagonal crack running from the top-left corner to the center
of the panel, with a smaller spiderweb fracture pattern radiating from
the impact point near the top edge. The display still powers on but
shows discoloration and a dark band across the cracked area, consistent
with internal panel damage from blunt impact.

Repair shop assessment:
The technician confirmed the damage is consistent with a drop/impact
event, not a manufacturing defect or wear-related issue. The LCD panel
requires full replacement. Estimated repair cost: $210.

Photos and the original repair invoice are on file with the claim
reference number above.
